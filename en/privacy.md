# Privacy Policy — Strikt

[Français](https://yannis1307.github.io/strikt-legal/confidentialite) · **English**

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Publisher: Yannis Leroy — strikt.contact@gmail.com

## What Strikt keeps on your iPhone

First name, age, goal, obstacles, estimate of lost hours, letter to your future self, chosen plan, habits, check-offs, streak, streak shields and settings. There is no account and no server for this data. It is erased when you uninstall the app.

## iCloud backup

If an iCloud account is signed in, Strikt backs up this data to your own iCloud space, at Apple, so you can get it back after a reinstall or on a new iPhone. We have no access to it. "Erase everything", in the app's Settings, also deletes this backup.

## Usage statistics (PostHog, servers in the European Union)

To improve the app, we measure screens viewed, steps completed, habits logged, questionnaire answers (age, goal, obstacle category, estimated hours) and subscription steps. PostHog also automatically receives the device model, iOS version, language, time zone, and app opens, backgrounding, installs and updates. No location is derived from your IP address. This data is tied to a random identifier, never to your first name. The letter to your future self is never sent.

## Subscriptions (Apple, RevenueCat)

Subscriptions go through the App Store and are managed by RevenueCat, which receives your purchases and your subscription (offer, trial, renewal), an anonymous identifier, your IP address and the country of your App Store account. We receive neither your name, nor your email, nor your payment details.

## What we don't do

No advertising, no ad tracking, no sale of data.

## Your rights

You can request access to, correction of or deletion of the data concerning you by writing to strikt.contact@gmail.com. You can also refer the matter to the CNIL (cnil.fr).

## Children

Strikt is not intended for children under 13.
