# Support — Strikt

[Français](https://yannis1307.github.io/strikt-legal/support) · **English**

A question, a problem, a request about your data? Write to us at **strikt.contact@gmail.com**. We usually reply within a few days.

## Frequently asked questions

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Strikt > Cancel.

**I changed phones, where are my habits?**
Strikt backs them up to your iCloud if you're signed in. Sign in to the same iCloud account, then open the app.

**How do I restore my purchase?**
On the subscription screen, tap "Restore purchases".

**How do I delete my data?**
In the app: Settings > "Erase everything" (also deletes the iCloud backup). For the anonymous statistics, write to us.
